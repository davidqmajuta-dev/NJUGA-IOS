NJUGA/CASINO Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

NJUGA/CASINO is designed to work offline. The game does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information.

Local settings

Game settings such as theme, sound preferences, voice preferences, animation settings, and rival difficulty are stored locally on your device using browser/app storage so the game can remember your choices.

Audio and voice

If you enable voice announcements, the app uses the device's available speech synthesis service. Voice output is generated by the device and is not uploaded by NJUGA/CASINO.

Third-party services

The packaged game does not include advertising, analytics, user accounts, or remote game servers.

Children

The game is intended as a general-audience entertainment game and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the contact details associated with the Google Play developer account for NJUGA/CASINO.

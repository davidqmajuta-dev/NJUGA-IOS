## Short description

## Full description
NJUGA/CASINO brings a fast, strategic card-table experience to Android with a clean mobile interface and an adaptive rival AI.

### Features
- Play completely offline.
- Challenge a rival AI across multiple difficulty levels.
- Capture, build and trail cards using the game's tactical rules.
- Track captured cards and scoring during the match.
- Choose from Classic Felt, Midnight Blue and Warm Wood table themes.
- Adjust animations, rival move speed, music and voice announcements.
- Haptic feedback is supported on compatible Android devices.
- Settings are saved locally on your device.
- Portrait-first interface designed for phone screens.

No account is required and the game contains no in-game betting or real-money wagering.

**Price:** Free (no in-app purchases, no ads in this build)
**Devices:** iPhone only

## Promotional text (170)
Capture, build and trail your way to victory in a polished offline card game with an adaptive rival - plus online rooms and tournaments with friends.

## Description
NJUGA/CASINO is a fast, beautiful card game for one player or friends.
- Play offline against a rival AI that adapts to how you play
- Online rooms: share a 5-letter code and play a friend
- Tournaments with buy-ins (play money)
- Three table themes: Felt, Midnight and Wood
- Haptic feedback, music and voice cues
- No account, no ads

Virtual chips only. No real money is ever used or won.

## Age rating (important)
The game has a virtual wallet and a casino theme. In App Store Connect's age-rating questionnaire answer **"Simulated Gambling: Infrequent/Mild or Frequent/Intense"** - this will give a **17+** rating. Never present chips as redeemable for real money. If you ever add real-money play, Apple requires gambling licences per country.

## App Review notes
"Offline card game with an AI opponent. Online rooms use a relay server (wss://njuga-relay.onrender.com) that only forwards game messages; no accounts or personal data. All currency is virtual."
Review-risk tip: make sure the relay server is **running** when Apple reviews, or the Online screen will look broken.

Data collected: **None.** Tracking: **No.** (The game stores settings and the play-money wallet locally on the device only.)
